Nursery – Grade 2

Foundation Years

Building Strong Foundations for Life

At Alagar Public School, we believe that the early years of education shape a child's confidence, curiosity and love for learning. Our Foundation Years Programme, designed for Nursery to Grade 2, provides a joyful and nurturing environment where every child feels safe, valued and inspired to explore.

Blending Montessori practices with experiential learning, we focus on developing the whole child — academically, socially, emotionally and creatively. Through storytelling, happy hours, hands-on activities, field trips and interactive classroom experiences, children learn with excitement and confidence every day.

Our programme emphasizes

  • check_circle Montessori-based learning
  • check_circle Storytelling & communication
  • check_circle Language & speaking skills
  • check_circle Social & life skills
  • check_circle Independent learning habits
  • check_circle Play-based & experiential activities
  • check_circle Exploration through field trips & creative engagement

At this stage, children are encouraged to ask questions, express themselves freely, collaborate with peers and develop essential skills for lifelong learning. We aim to nurture happy, confident and independent learners who are ready to grow, explore and soar.

Learn • Explore • Grow • Lead — Building a Strong Foundation, One Step at a Time.

Grade 3 – 5

Discoverers

Building Future-Ready Explorers

At Alagar Public School, the years from Grades 3 to 5 mark an exciting phase of discovery, growth and transformation. As learners become more independent and curious about the world around them, our programme is designed to strengthen academic foundations while nurturing creativity, confidence and character.

Through engaging classroom experiences, hands-on learning and meaningful exploration, students are encouraged to think critically, ask questions, solve problems and collaborate effectively. We believe learning should go beyond textbooks — inspiring children to become confident explorers and responsible individuals.

Grade 9 – 12

Trailblazers

Building Pathways to Success

At Alagar Public School, the senior school years (Grades 9 to 12) represent the pinnacle of school life. Our programme is designed to guide students towards academic excellence, career readiness and global citizenship, preparing them to enter the world as confident trailblazers.

With a rigorous curriculum, advanced learning facilities, career guidance and comprehensive support, we empower our seniors to achieve their dreams, stand out and lead — including integrated NEET / IIT preparation alongside the CBSE curriculum.
