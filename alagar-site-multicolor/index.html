Welcome to Alagar

A school every child, parent & teacher loves

At Alagar Public School, happiness is at the heart of learning. We believe great results are the natural outcome of a joyful childhood — not the price of a stressful one. Every child is known by name, challenged at their own pace and free to discover what they are truly capable of.

Established in 2009 by the Alagar Charitable Foundation, we deliver world-class CBSE education with a focus on holistic development, character and academic excellence — right here in Thoothukudi.
