Field Trips & Camps

Where strangers become friends, and friendships last forever

Field Trips

The school bus buzzes with excited chatter as students head off to new destinations — science centres, salt factories, harbours and agricultural farms. Tied to the curriculum, these outings teach real-world lessons while strengthening friendships.

forest

Camps

Camping is a powerful teacher. Through climbing walls, rappelling down rock cliffs and navigating obstacle courses, students build self-awareness, resilience and friendships — growing in confidence away from home.
